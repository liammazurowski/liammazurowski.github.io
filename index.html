About

I am currently an Assistant Professor at Southern Connecticut State University. Previously, I was a C.C. Hsiung Postdoctoral Research Associate at Lehigh University mentored by Ao Sun, and an H.C. Wang Assistant Professor at Cornell University mentored by Xin Zhou. I received my PhD from the University of Chicago in 2021. My advisor was André Neves.

Research

My research lies in differential geometry, geometric analysis, and PDEs. I am especially interested in prescribed mean curvature surfaces, scalar curvature, and the p-Laplacian.
